FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp — we'll answer straight.

Do I need a fiscal device to comply with ZIMRA?

Not necessarily. ZIMRA allows a virtual fiscal device connected through its API, so your system can issue fiscal invoices without extra hardware. On the free check we confirm what fits your setup.

Which currencies can I invoice in?

USD and ZiG for Zimbabwe — including split-currency invoices — and PLN and EUR for Poland. We agree a payment method that works for you, such as bank transfer or mobile money where available.

What happens when ZIMRA or KSeF rules change?

With Monitored Care we track rule changes and update your system. Without it, updates are quoted as small fixed-price jobs.

What about power cuts and bad internet?

We design for them: work is saved locally and syncs when the connection returns, and WhatsApp messages wait in a queue until they can send.

Where is my data stored?

On established cloud infrastructure — we work mainly with Microsoft Azure — in a region we agree with you. It's your data: you can export it any time.

Do you only work in Zimbabwe and Poland?

They're our focus, but automations and dashboards work anywhere. As other countries bring in e-invoicing — Germany, France and Nigeria are next — we follow with the same approach.

Is the 30-minute check really free?

Yes, and there's no pitch. We look at how you invoice and operate and tell you what's worth fixing first. If it's a fit, a paid Blueprint turns it into a fixed quote.
